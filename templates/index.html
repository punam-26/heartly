Heart health check

Check heart disease risk from 13 health measurements

Enter a patient's resting and exercise-test results. Heartly compares them with 302 real patient records and tells you whether the pattern looks low, moderate or high risk.

Make a prediction See the dashboard

How it works

Three steps, about a minute.

  1. Enter the measurementsAge, blood pressure, cholesterol, heart rate, ECG and scan results. Or load an example record.
  2. Heartly finds similar patientsIt looks for the 15 patients in the data whose results are closest to yours.
  3. You get a risk statusLow, moderate or high, plus how your numbers compare with patients who do and do not have the disease.
